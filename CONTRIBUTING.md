# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this list. Pull requests from anyone are welcome, including additions, fixes, and removals of dead or outdated links.

## What belongs here

- Material specifically about Forward Deployed Engineering: the role, how teams run it, hiring, interviews, and the skills the work needs.
- Primary sources first: writing by companies that run FDE teams, talks by people who do or lead the work, and official hiring guidance.
- Free to access. Partly paywalled articles are fine if they are marked as such.

## What does not belong here

- Paid courses, coaching, bootcamps, or cohort programs.
- Individual job postings. Link the company's careers page instead, since postings close.
- General AI or software engineering material that is not clearly useful for FDE work. Other lists cover this well.
- Pages that mainly exist to collect emails or sell something.
- AI-generated listicles with no first-hand information.

## How to add an entry

1. One link per pull request, so each can be reviewed on its own.
2. Put it in the most fitting section, at the end of that section unless order clearly matters.
3. Use this format:

   ```
   - [Title (Source)](https://link) - One short, factual line on what it covers.
   ```

4. Describe what the resource is, not how good it is. Skip words like "amazing," "must-read," or "ultimate."
5. Check that the link works and is not a redirect to a different page.
6. In the pull request, say in a sentence why it is useful for someone doing or seeking FDE work. If you wrote it or work for the company behind it, say so. That is fine; we just want to know.

## Removing or fixing entries

Dead links, moved pages, and outdated descriptions can be fixed in any pull request. Group several fixes together if you like.

## Review

Maintainers review for relevance, accuracy, and fit. We may edit descriptions for length and tone. Not every suggestion will be added, and we will explain why when we decline.
